Round robin settings

Who is in the rotation, who is out of office, and whether leads are assigned automatically.

Automatic assignment

New contacts in the HubSpot round robin list get an owner within a couple of minutes.

Rotation

Leads and booking link visitors go to each rep in this order. Reps who are off or out of office are skipped.

Round robin booking link

One link for every customer. It opens the right rep's meeting link.

  • A contact with an owner goes to their owner's meeting link, unless that rep is out of office or has no link.
  • Anyone else goes to whoever is next in the rotation (or boosted) and has a meeting link. Visits don't move the rotation; only assigned leads do.
  • The same email opening it again within a day goes back to the same rep. Each visit is on the report.
  • The scheduler opens with their name and email filled in, from email, firstname and lastname on the link or from HubSpot.
